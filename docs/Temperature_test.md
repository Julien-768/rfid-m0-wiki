# Temperature measurement

This page describes the tests realized with the embedded temperature sensor (RTD sensor based on a PT100).

## Measurement comparison

The temperature measurement of the system was compared with another temperature sensor in a controlled temperature test chamber. The two sensors were inserted into the test chamber, with a temperature setting from 25°C to 45°c with 2.5°c step :

<a href="../assets/images/User_description/Temp_measurement_Wacu_VS_Burrow_enclosure.jpg">
<img alt="Measurement report of the temperature between a WACU and a the RTD with a PT100" src="../assets/images/User_description/Temp_measurement_Wacu_VS_Burrow_enclosure.jpg" width="600">
</a>

Results indicates that the gap between the two sensors is quite acceptable (~0.1°c). In addition the temperature difference between the two sensors and the settings can be explained by the temperature regulation of the test chamber, as the temperature overshoot.

Note : the inertia of the WACU logger can be explained by the specific mounting around the sensors during the test (plastic case)

Equipment used :

- Second temperature sensor : [MS5541-30](https://disensors.com/product/ms5541-30c-pressure-sensor/) (Sensor that equipped the WACU loggers of the MIBE)
- Test chamber : [DY60T](https://www.directindustry.com/prod/angelantoni-test-technologies/product-18270-1975819.html) humidity and temperature test chamber

## Temperature measurement inside the case

The temperature inside the burrow case was monitored during one month from the 20/06/2023 to the 19/07/2023.
Measurements indicates that the temperature inside the case does not exceed 53°C for a max outdoor temperature of 36.4°c, which is an acceptable temperature for the components.
