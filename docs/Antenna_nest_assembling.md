# Make the coil antenna

This page describes how to prepare a coil antenna intended to be installed into a nest box.

Refers to the [dedicated page](./Antenna_schwegler.md) for the antenna description with its specifications.

- **3D print** the part « Antenna.stl »
- [Cable a coil antenna](./Assembling_antenna.md) for a 192uH impedance (~67 spires) around the antenna base

<a href="../assets/images/Antennas/Nest_box_antenna_wiring.jpg">
<img alt="Nestbox antenna wired around a 3D printed part" src="../assets/images/Antennas/Nest_box_antenna_wiring.jpg" height="400" width="400">
</a>

- **Cable the IR** emitters and receivers with 28 AWG wire (provide 25cm of free length). Ensure to respect the cable colors :

<a href="../assets/images/Antennas/Nest_box_IR_wiring.jpg">
<img alt="infrared emitters and photo-receivers wired for 3D printed part" src="../assets/images/Antennas/Nest_box_IR_wiring.jpg" height="400" width="400">
</a>

| Color  | Function      |
| ------ | ------------- |
| Yellow | Receiver IR 1 |
| Yellow | Receiver IR 2 |
| Blue   | Emitter IR 1  |
| Blue   | Emitter IR 2  |
| Brown  | +3.3V         |
| Black  | GND           |

- **Stick the IR** into the antenna :
  - Position the IR Emitting Diode on the left side, seated in the hole and block it with glue gun
  - Position the IR receiver module on the right side, face to the hole and block it with glue gun.
